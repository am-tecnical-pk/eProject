SYSTEM EXECUTION CYCLE

From Ingestion to Executive Action

01

Ingest & Cleanse

Datasets are ingested through CSV upload or live database tables. Null and corrupted records are handled gracefully through mean/mode statistical imputation.

02

MapReduce Filter

Python mapper workers process student cohorts concurrently, isolating threshold outliers (attendance < 75%, score degradation > 25%) into reduced anomaly vectors.

03

ML Predictive Scoring

Features are passed to pre-trained estimators, outputting individual dropout risk classifications (Low, Medium, High) alongside predicted course demands.

04

Multi-Role Visualization

Inferences are delivered dynamically to specialized RBAC interfaces: Administrators manage records, Teachers view batch risks, and Students track personal grades.

05

Automated Email Alerting

Whenever an analyst completes a report or a critical risk anomaly is detected, an automated alert email is compiled and dispatched directly to the desired stakeholder.

06

Intervention & Retention

Academic advisors initiate targeted mentoring prior to final examinations, directly addressing the core institutional problem statement of declining retention.
